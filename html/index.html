About the app

Classroom Checker is a desktop application that helps teachers review their Google Classroom coursework and identify student submissions that have not been graded yet.

What it does

After the teacher signs in with their Google account, the app reads relevant Google Classroom data and displays ungraded student submissions for coursework created by the signed-in teacher.

Google Classroom data used

The app may access Google Classroom courses, coursework, student submissions, and student profile information only for the purpose of showing the teacher which submitted assignments are not graded yet.

Data handling

Classroom Checker does not sell user data, does not share Google user data with third parties, and does not use Google user data for advertising.

OAuth tokens are stored locally on the user's computer and are used only to access Google Classroom on behalf of the signed-in user.
